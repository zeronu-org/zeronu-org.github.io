# ZeroNu

We ask a simple question.  Why can’t we have the experimental answer tomorrow?

Neutrinoless double beta decay ($0\nu\beta\beta$) is not blocked by a lack of ideas.  It is blocked by a lack of decisive execution at scale.

:::commentary
**What counts as decisive?**
A decisive result means the experiment is large enough, clean enough, and stable enough that a null result genuinely closes the window, not just narrows it.
:::

## The thesis

Modern $0\nu\beta\beta$ experiments are not “killer idea” problems.  They are integration hell problems.

Excellent energy resolution has been demonstrated.  Low-background materials have been demonstrated.  Background rejection has been demonstrated.  Topological discrimination has been demonstrated.  Daughter identification has been demonstrated.  However, these capabilities do not yet exist simultaneously in one detector, at scale.

## Scale is the king

Scale is where backgrounds become statistically decisive and discovery becomes unavoidable.  Scale is also where engineering, procurement, QA, and schedule discipline dominate outcomes.

Scale requires capital.  Capital is not an auxiliary activity.  It is an integral part of engineering execution.

:::commentary
**Funding is part of the system**
Treat funding like any other subsystem: owners, milestones, and integration points with engineering and procurement.
:::
