# Join the side channel

ZeroNu is for people who want the $0\nu\beta\beta$ answer badly enough to build a new channel for it.

We are forming a nonprofit to concentrate capital, authority, and full-time technical effort on one mission: construct and commission a decisive neutrinoless double beta decay experiment.

## Pledge resources

The first need is committed capital. This includes philanthropic gifts, personal pledges, company proceeds, equipment, facilities, or bridge resources that reduce schedule risk.

If you are evaluating a pledge, ask for:

- funding threshold and spending rules;
- governance and decision rights;
- milestone schedule and reporting cadence;
- technical risk register;
- how funds map to staffing, procurement, assay, and commissioning.

:::commentary
**The right donor**

This project is not looking for symbolic support. It is looking for people who understand that the funding model is the technical bottleneck and want their capital to change the operating model.
:::

## Join the core team

The core team must be small, expert, and full time. The program needs people who can own complete systems, not just advise from the edge.

Needed capabilities include:

- detector design and low-background construction;
- radiopurity, assay, procurement, and contamination control;
- electronics, controls, DAQ, and monitoring;
- calibration, reconstruction, statistics, and open-data infrastructure;
- mechanical integration, operations, safety, and QA.

:::commentary
**The commitment is severe**

The intended commitment is five years of full-time work. That is the point. Fractional effort is the disease this project is built to escape.
:::

## Challenge the plan

Serious criticism is welcome. The side-channel model must survive technical attack before it deserves capital.

Useful criticism is specific:

- What assumption breaks first at scale?
- Which background cannot be controlled?
- Which procurement or QA step will dominate schedule?
- Where does the five-year plan underestimate integration?
- What measurement would prove the approach is working?
