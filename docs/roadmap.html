Roadmap

This roadmap targets a decisive 0νββ measurement on a five‑year build‑and‑commission timeline after full funding is secured. The phases are engineered to eliminate integration risk step by step, not simply to grow detector mass.

Phase 0: Funding lock and governance

  • Establish the nonprofit, legal structure, and decision authority.
  • Secure the full capital threshold that enables end‑to‑end execution.
  • Publish the schedule, staffing plan, and success metrics.

Phase 1: Integrated module demo

  • Combine energy resolution, backgrounds, topology, and stability in one module.
  • Define the full calibration and QA pipeline early.
  • Demonstrate repeatability and measurable yield.

Phase 2: Multi‑module system test

  • Validate services, calibration, uptime model, and data chain end‑to‑end.
  • Prove that integration scales without runaway complexity.
  • Stress‑test maintenance and failure recovery.

Phase 3: Pilot‑scale deployment

  • Operate at realistic scale with production materials and real QA flows.
  • Freeze the design for procurement and manufacturing.
  • Validate background models with real operations data.

Phase 4: Full‑scale build and commissioning

  • Execute procurement, logistics, installation, and commissioning.
  • Publish first open‑data release once stable operations are achieved.
  • Transition to lean, long‑term operations.
Schedule discipline is a deliverable

Each phase must end with an integrated demonstration that de‑risks the next scale step. If it cannot be integrated and scheduled, it does not count.

Open data expectations

Open data begins once the experiment is stable and calibrated, with documentation sufficient for external analysis.
